MCU Atlas helps you explore MCU watch orders, playlists, and characters, track what you've already watched, and keep your own independent saved places — right on your device.

What the app does

MCU Atlas presents a reference catalog of MCU titles, episodes, and character appearances, and lets you mark what you've watched yourself. It does not stream or play video, and it does not check streaming availability in real time — "Where to watch" destinations are recorded in the catalog with their own source and verification date, and open the provider's own app or website.

Progress tracking

Marking something watched is always an explicit action you take. Opening a "Where to watch" destination never marks anything watched, for any provider. MCU Atlas does not import progress from a streaming provider's own history — there is no such integration.

When the catalog is updated, entries are designed to keep their identity so your progress carries forward, but this is a design goal rather than an absolute guarantee for every possible edit.

Spoiler choice

The first time you open the app, you choose between two equally weighted options before seeing any content — neither is preselected. If you'd rather see everything immediately, choose the "show everything" option. You can revisit this choice later in Settings.

iCloud sync

Private iCloud synchronization across your devices is planned for MCU Atlas, using your own Apple Account and Apple's private CloudKit database — there is no server operated by the developer. Syncing needs an iCloud account signed in on your devices; the app is fully usable without one, and your progress is kept on the device in that case. An iCloud account is a prerequisite only for keeping devices in sync, not for using the app.

Provider links, Handoff, and Spotlight

Provider links
"Where to watch" destinations open the streaming provider's own app or website. MCU Atlas does not track what happens after you leave the app.
Handoff
With Handoff turned on, the title, route, or search you're viewing can be picked up by your own nearby Apple devices signed into the same Apple Account.
Spotlight
Spotlight on your device is given public catalog titles and curated route names only — never your watch history, notes, or searches.

Known limits

  • Progress tracking is manual; there is no automatic import from any streaming provider.
  • The app does not check live streaming availability — "Where to watch" entries are catalog-recorded, not looked up at runtime.
  • Catalog updates are designed to preserve your progress, but this is not an absolute guarantee in every case.
  • MCU Atlas does not use Live Activities.
  • MCU Atlas is an unofficial fan reference. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Marvel or The Walt Disney Company.
